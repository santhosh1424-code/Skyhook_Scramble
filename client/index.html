Explorer’s Guide — Treasure Isle

1. Follow the Clues: Your compass at the top points toward your current clue (caves, ruins, shipwrecks, camps). Press E (or DIG/E) near a clue scroll or carved X stone to unlock the next riddle.
2. Dig Up the Chest: Once near the carved X rock, use your Shovel (E) to dig! Watch for warm/cold soil feedback. Unearthing the chest takes two digs.
3. Fight & Deliver: Carrying the heavy chest slows you down. Swing your Cutlass (F / Click) or Shove (X) to knock rivals down and steal the chest, then carry it back to your Ship Berth!
4. Customize Anytime: Open your Wardrobe (C) when standing next to your ship dock or any camp chest!
