How to Play Skyhook Scramble

1. Grab the Storm Core: Run (WASD), Jump (Space), and Ledge-Climb up island rims to reach the central shrine when the 3-2-1 countdown ends.
2. Grapple & Dash: Aim near a highlighted sky-buoy or mast (◎) and press Right Click or E (Controller: LT/LB) to zip across gaps. Press Shift (or Left Click) to Dash or knock the Core loose!
3. Survive Core Mutations: Every 30s a 5-second warning announces a Core Mutation (Floaty, Bouncy, Lightning Magnet, Decoy Split). Use your Gadget (Q / F / Controller X) to escape or steal!
4. Deliver to Your Airship: Jump into your airship's glowing ring to score +1. Tied at 0:00? Sudden-Death Overtime!
